The Core Tension

A textile manufacturer, a chemical plant, a steel mill — they all know clean energy is the future. But when the board meets, the choice is always the same:

Deploy ₹50 Crore into expanding production capacity? Or into solar panels?

Every business will prioritise core operations over sustainability capex. Not because they don't care — because capital is finite and production comes first.

"The gap isn't awareness. The gap is capital allocation. Industries don't need more reasons to go green — they need someone to remove the capex barrier entirely."

The Singapore advantage

Why Singapore Capital Belongs in Indian Renewables

Double-Digit Effective USD Yields

Indian solar delivers ~14–15% IRR in INR terms. Even after 3–4% annual currency depreciation, effective USD returns of 10–11% significantly outperform most global infrastructure alternatives.

India-Singapore DTAA Benefits

The Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement ensures tax-efficient structuring. Capital gains and dividend taxation are optimised under the bilateral treaty framework.

Full Repatriation Under FEMA

Dividends and capital gains from Indian solar SPVs are fully repatriable via RBI's automatic route. No special approvals, no lock-in period. Well-established regulatory pathway.

Aligned with MAS Green Finance Framework

Singapore's Green Finance Action Plan and MAS sustainability taxonomy actively encourage green infrastructure investments. Indian solar aligns perfectly with ESG mandates and impact reporting.

Asset-Backed, Sovereign Counter-Party

Investments secured against physical infrastructure. Power purchase agreements with creditworthy industrial consumers or state government entities. Sovereign credit risk near-zero.
