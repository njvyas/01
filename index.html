Assumptions, sources and items to verify

  • Verify: open access charges are defaults to check against current MERC orders and MSEDCL/STU notifications before quoting. Wheeling ₹0.81/kWh is the HT wheeling rate printed on the customer's FY 2026-27 bills. Transmission ₹0.40/kWh is an industry estimate; MERC's InSTS order (Case 208 of 2024) sets long-term charges in ₹/kW/month. Wheeling loss 6% and transmission loss 3% are conservative assumptions; published HT wheeling-loss figures vary widely between sources.
  • Cross-subsidy surcharge and additional surcharge are taken as nil for a compliant group captive (≥26% equity, ≥51% consumption). A grid-connected plant may avoid MSEDCL wheeling if it injects into the STU network and the consumer is EHV-connected; MERC has ruled so in several cases. This customer draws at 33 kV, so wheeling is retained by default.
  • MERC's draft 2026 open access regulations propose banking and standby charges (working-group base case ₹139/kW/month). The banking/grid-support input is set to nil and should be revisited once the regulations are final.
  • Energy model: a daily balance per month. Solar first meets the factory's daytime load, the surplus charges the BESS, and the BESS discharges at night up to its usable capacity. Surplus beyond that is curtailed, with no banking credit. Monthly solar shape is an inferred Marathwada fixed-tilt profile; replace it with PVsyst output once a site is chosen.
  • Savings use the full ₹11.22/kWh average grid cost, as instructed. This overstates savings: MSEDCL demand charges (₹45–51 lakh/month on 9,500 kVA), the region subsidy and the incremental consumption rebate do not all scale down with open access units.
  • The existing 2.375 MW behind-the-meter rooftop plant is excluded; the target applies to grid-metered consumption only.
  • Tax: corporate rate and 40% WDV depreciation on the full capitalised block, with losses carried forward. Replacement capex (inverters, BESS augmentation) is added to the block in the year incurred. GST rates on solar EPC (composite) and BESS should be confirmed with tax advisers.
  • Customer dividends assume all positive equity cash flow is distributed pro rata to shareholding.
  • Alendei margin is modelled as a per-unit fee paid by the SPV to Alendei (for example under an O&M or asset-management agreement). It is a tax-deductible SPV cost, so the tariff solve recovers it and the payback target still holds. Alendei benefit = its equity share of SPV equity cash flow + margin income − its equity investment. Margin income is shown pre-tax at Alendei level.
  • The recommendation applies fixed rules: customer saving, project payback, Alendei IRR, minimum DSCR and RE share against the criteria set in the inputs. It is a screening aid, not an investment decision.
  • These figures are for internal pricing decisions; verify with a qualified professional before acting.
